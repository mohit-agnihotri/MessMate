Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 • Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 & Google Play Developer Policies

Welcome to Mezzo ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of all personal and financial data entrusted to us by mess owners, hostel wardens, and dining students.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to facilitate mess meal planning, automated attendance cutoffs, transparent ledger tracking, and monthly billing:

  • Account Information: Name, mobile phone number, email address, and role (Student or Mess Owner) provided during authentication via Google Sign-In or Firebase Phone OTP.
  • Mess & Meal Records: Daily menu items, meal statuses (present, self-absent, guest meals, owner-marked leaves), and cutoff notification settings.
  • Financial & Billing Data: Monthly generated bill records, paid/unpaid status, and kitchen expense ledger records (Sabzi, Doodh, Ration purchases). Note: Mezzo does NOT store credit/debit card numbers or UPI banking PINs. All payments are settled directly between parties.
  • Device & Location Data: Precise foreground GPS coordinates are requested strictly when students use the "Discover Nearby Messes" map screen to locate dining facilities within a 5km radius. We never track or record background location.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Enabling mess owners to publish daily menus and calculate exact headcount for meal prep.
  • Enabling students to skip meals before automated cutoff deadlines to avoid food and fee wastage.
  • Generating transparent, dispute-free monthly bills and downloadable receipts.
  • Sending real-time push notifications regarding join approvals, meal schedule updates, and bill dues via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

3. Data Protection & Security

All data transmitted between the Mezzo mobile application and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Stored records are safeguarded by Google Cloud Firestore using AES-256 encryption at rest, protected by strict server-side Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) rules.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to any advertising brokers or data mining companies. Data is shared strictly with essential service providers necessary to operate the platform:

  • Google Firebase: Cloud database, authentication, and push notification infrastructure.
  • Cloudinary: Compressed delivery of user-uploaded mess menu and dish images.

5. Your Rights & Permanent Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data under Indian DPDP Act 2023. You may at any time:

  • Request an export or review of your meal attendance and billing history.
  • Request permanent erasure of your account, student profile, or mess records directly inside the app (under Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account) or via our public Account Deletion Request Page.
