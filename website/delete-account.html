Mezzo Privacy & Data Governance • Google Play Policy Compliant
🔒 Direct In-App Deletion Available

If you have access to the Mezzo mobile app, you can delete your account instantly by navigating to Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account.

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can use the form below to submit a formal account and data deletion request directly to our engineering team.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

  • Your profile information (Full Name, Phone Number, Email, Profile Avatar).
  • Your authentication record in Firebase Auth.
  • Your active student enrollment or mess registration records.
  • Historical meal attendance logs and skip records.

Note: Completed bills and past transaction invoices may be retained in anonymized format for statutory tax and ledger compliance as required under applicable laws.

✅ Your deletion request has been submitted successfully. Your account and associated data will be permanently purged within 48 to 72 hours.
