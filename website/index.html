FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Does Mezzo charge any commission on student UPI payments?

+

No, absolutely zero percent (0%). When students scan your mess QR code or tap to pay fees via UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM), the payment routes directly into your own personal or business bank account. Mezzo does not hold or deduct from your money.

How does the 30-minute cancellation cutoff work?

+

Each meal slot has a cutoff time (e.g., 12:00 PM for Lunch, 07:00 PM for Dinner). If a student marks "Skip Meal" before this cutoff, their meal deduction is recorded and the kitchen headcount updates instantly. Once the cutoff passes, cancellations are locked to prevent food preparation wastage.

Is student and mess owner data secure and private?

+

Yes. Mezzo is engineered with strict role-based data isolation and conforms to the India DPDP Act 2023. Other mess owners can never view your financial ledger, students, or rates. Furthermore, both owners and students have a 1-click Right to Erasure to wipe their accounts whenever they choose.

How do students install the app if it's not on the Play Store yet?

+

Students can directly download and install our official Android APK file from this website or scan the QR code printed on your mess standee. The installation takes less than 30 seconds on any Android phone running Android 7.0 or above.
